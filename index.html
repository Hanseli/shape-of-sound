THE SURVIVOR FILTER

Popularity is a signal from now,
not a chart from then.

Spotify's popularity score (0–100) describes a track's current standing on the platform. It is not a reconstruction of its historical chart rank. A high score for a song released decades ago is a trace of its present-day listening life: an old recording still circulating in a modern streaming catalog.

We call that a survivor. The Survivor Filter compares high-popularity older tracks with faded tracks from the same decade, then sets their audio fingerprints beside contemporary music. It can reveal patterns in this catalog—but it cannot tell us why a listener presses play.

THE SURVIVOR FILTER, IN SHORT

A current popularity score can point us toward songs that still circulate. Audio features can describe their sound. Neither can tell the whole story of memory, rediscovery, licensing, playlist placement, or the people who keep listening.
